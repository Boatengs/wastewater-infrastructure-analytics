# Utility Case Studies

## Purpose
Use verified public utility examples to connect asset-risk analytics with real rehabilitation, replacement, capacity, resilience, and capital-delivery decisions.

## Status
No wastewater utility case study is represented here as verified yet. This file establishes the evidence standard before case studies are added.

## Minimum case-study evidence
For each selected utility or project, document:
- system and asset context
- problem/condition evidence
- intervention selected and alternatives considered
- project scale and relevant capacity/flow
- published capital cost and estimate year, when available
- O&M implications, when available
- funding source and project status
- source documents and dates
- what is observed fact versus project interpretation

## Candidate case-study themes
- interceptor or trunk sewer rehabilitation
- pump-station renewal or redundancy
- force-main failure-risk management
- inflow/infiltration reduction programs
- consent-decree or SSO-driven capital programs
- treatment-plant process renewal
- climate/flood resilience investments

All case-study facts must be traceable through `sources/SOURCE_REGISTER.md` before being described as verified.
